\section{Technical Feasibility}

Finding cycles in directed graphs is a well-studied problem. For small loop sizes (up to 4 people), a Depth-First Search (DFS) algorithm works fast and efficiently for up to 1,000 users \cite{johnson1975finding}. Using a synthetic data generator ensures we can test and demonstrate the system easily even without real users.

\hline

\hline

No Real Users Yet & Synthetic data generator creates test users & Fully Solved \\
Demonstration Risk & Pre-made test data guarantees trade loops for demo & Fully Solved \\
